ROOTED IN KASHMIR

From the valley
to the wider world.

Civara Workshop is built on a long relationship with Kashmir’s people, textiles and ways of making. The founder’s working life began young, learning through experience, trade and travel rather than a conventional classroom. Over decades in export and handicrafts, one lesson remained: a product travels best when its story and details travel with it.

01 · THE CRAFT

Respect the work

Pashmina, Sozani, Kani, Jamawar and wool carry distinctive skills and histories. Our responsibility is to describe each item carefully, work with makers and producers, and be clear about materials and evidence.

02 · THE CONNECTION

Trade can build relationships

Years of working with international buyers have taught us the value of clear communication, reliable details and long-term relationships. We want every enquiry to begin with understanding, not exaggerated promises.

03 · THE PURPOSE

Business with a wider heart

The journey to Zanskar and the offering of woolen robes at Karsha strengthened a vision in which craft, hospitality and education can support a more connected community.
