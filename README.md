OUR VISION

To make the world understand Pashmina before it buys Pashmina.

Civara Workshop exists to connect people with the material, knowledge and human craftsmanship behind Kashmir's Pashmina tradition.

Through our Pashmina Knowledge Centre, we document and explain the journey of the fibre — its Himalayan origin, its transformation into yarn and cloth, the craftspeople who work with it, the traditions that have developed around it, and the evidence that helps us understand authenticity.

Our vision is not to turn Kashmir's craft into a museum piece.

It is to help a living tradition remain understood, respected and connected to the people who carry it forward.
