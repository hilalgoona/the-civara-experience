A JOURNEY THROUGH THE MOUNTAINS

From Kashmir
to Zanskar and beyond.

Between 13 and 31 August 2026, our journey travelled from Srinagar through Zanskar, Nubra Valley, Pangong Lake and Leh, alongside friends from different parts of the world. The places were extraordinary; the people and moments of connection made the journey meaningful.

ROBES OF COMPASSION

Warmth offered with care.

The offering of 50 woolen robes in Karsha inspired a larger vision: “1000 Robes for Compassion.” The aim is to support future offerings through thoughtful collaboration and transparent communication.

These robes are described as woolen and made with care; we do not describe them as handmade.
