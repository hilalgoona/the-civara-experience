THE CHAI MODEL

Education that
opens possibilities.

ClimbingHi Academic Institute (CHAI) grew from a simple belief: children deserve access to learning, and a stronger community grows when education is connected with family wellbeing and meaningful livelihood opportunities.

01

Education

A vision for accessible learning and support for children, with a focus on building strong foundations and confidence.

02

Family Development

Working alongside families and recognising the practical challenges that can affect a child’s opportunity to learn.

03

Enterprise

Connecting community development with skills, local craft and opportunities for sustainable livelihoods.

A COMMUNITY-ROOTED VISION

Small beginnings.
A wider hope.

The project began with a home-based learning effort for neighbourhood children and a goal of making education more accessible. Our next steps depend on resources, local needs and the support we can responsibly build.

We will share confirmed programme details and current opportunities here as the project develops. Children’s identities and private information will always be protected.
