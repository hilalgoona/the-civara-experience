FIELD NOTE 01 ZANSKAR · 2026

ROBES OF COMPASSION

Warmth offered with care.

During a journey through Zanskar and Ladakh in August 2026, 50 woollen monastic robes were offered to nuns at Karsha Monastery. It was a meaningful moment behind a wider aspiration: to work towards offering 1,000 robes over time, with care and community support.

The 1,000-robe vision is a future goal, not a completed delivery.

FIELD NOTE 02KASHMIR · CRAFT

BEHIND THE WORK

Craft deserves clarity.

Pashmina, Kani, Jamawar and Sozani each carry distinct traditions and techniques. We believe buyers should be able to understand what a product is made from, how it was produced, and what evidence supports its description. We aim to present those details plainly rather than ask customers to rely on beautiful words alone.

Learning should open doors.
